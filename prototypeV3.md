## META

### Core Purpose

Create a persistent world [^persistence] roguelike[^roguelite] deckbuilder[^deckbuilder] based on [the theme from Shelder Evolution](#the-premise), that adheres to the [minimum viable product UX requirements](#mvp).

[^persistence]: A persistent world exists independently of the player. In the case of a single-player game, the world lives in the savegame, while in the case of a MMO, the world lives on the server. See [persistence](./docs/spec/persistence.md) for more.

[^roguelite]: a sub-genre of videogames that has some elements of a 'roguelike', like procedurally generated maps, perma-death, resource management, etc. See ['Roguelike VS. Roguelite - The SIMPLEST Explanation' by Dorian The Explorian](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RERyFXXHjsU&t=1s). See [roguelite deckbuilder](./docs/spec/roguelike_deckbuilder.md) for how ShelderEvo employs 'roguelite' mechanics.

[^deckbuilder]: a genre of card games where the main focus is to build a unique deck by collecting cards through the game. The gameplay might be focused around optimizing the deck for a certain playstyle and to achieve specific goals. See ['What makes a Great Deckbuilder' by Adam Millard - The Architect of Games](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d_gBRNAgBKQ), or ['
What Makes A Good DECK BUILDING Roguelike?' by PepperHead](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UG_T3S6-qUw&t=440s), or see [roguelite deckbuilder](./docs/spec/roguelite_deckbuilder.md) for how ShelderEvo employs 'deckbuilder' mechanics.

### MVP

The (M)inimum (V)iable (P)roduct must provide the following feelings of user experience (UX):

- [entities are persistent and perpetual](./docs/spec/persistence.md)
- [creatures are alive and growing](./docs/spec/lifecycle.md)
- [creatures can be adopted to follow their growth until death](./docs/spec/creature_adoption.md)
- [all creatures have their own valid agency and intelligence](./docs/spec/creature_agency.md)
- [affinity between player and creature as a balancing mechanic](./docs/spec/creature_affinity.md)
- ['bleak character agency'](./docs/spec/meta.md#agency)
- ['grey decisions'](./docs/spec/meta.md#tone)
- [plausible hard-sci-fi](./docs/spec/meta.md#genre)
- [sense of adventure](./docs/spec/quests.md)
- [emerging stories](./docs/spec/lore.md)
- [meta-narrative](./docs/spec/meta.md#meta-game)
- [no 'plot armor', no 'power creep', no 'balancing', no 'pay-to-win'](./docs/spec/meta.md#meta-balancing)
